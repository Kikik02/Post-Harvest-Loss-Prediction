# Post-Harvest-Loss-Prediction — Product Pitch

**Problem:** agricultural decisions can suffer from delayed or fragmented information.

**Solution:** an AI-enabled prototype focused on post harvest loss prediction.

**MVP:** data input, prediction/scoring, risk indicator, recommendation and analytics.

**Business routes:** farmer/FPO subscription, institutional licensing, advisory integration or API.

**Research extensions:** IoT, weather/satellite data, XAI, field validation, multi-season experiments and economic-impact analysis.
